## 1. Executive Summary & Deliverables

This quotation covers the end-to-end development, configuration, and 2-month cloud deployment of the **Arabic E-Badge Creation & Attendance Verification Web Application** for the **Ummu Habeeba Virtual Campus Graduation Ceremony 2026**.

### 🌟 Features Included:
* **Custom Arabic & English Dual-Language Registration Form**: Full support for student details, role tags, batch, year, class, and course.
* **High-DPI E-Badge Canvas Generator (8K Print Quality)**: Sleek dark navy & soft gold luxury design with circular avatar frame, subtle book, pen, and Arabic calligraphy watermarks.
* **Interactive Photo Crop Tool**: Integrated cropper for fitting profile photos into the badge avatar ring.
* **Instant Dynamic QR Code Engine**: Generates a unique registration code (e.g., `UHV-849201`) and embeds a scannable QR code onto every badge.
* **High-Resolution Exports**: Instant **PNG** & print-ready **PDF** downloads + direct **WhatsApp Sharing**.
* **Admin Applicant Data Table (`/data-table/`)**: Searchable participant database with batch filters, count stats, badge preview modal, and **UTF-8 Arabic Excel CSV Export**.
* **Web Camera QR Scanner (`/scanner/`)**: Live camera attendance scanner for venue check-in.
* **Public QR Verification Page (`/verify/<code>/`)**: Instant authenticity verification landing page.
